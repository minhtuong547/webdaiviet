# Phase 4.4 Design Research

## Scope And Method

This is art-direction research, not a component catalogue. The aim is to learn how premium game sites establish a world before asking a visitor to navigate it, then translate those methods into an original DAI VIET system.

Research was performed on 2026-10-21 using official pages where the page content was accessible. Diablo IV, Monster Hunter Wilds, and the two Awwwards URLs named in the brief returned reader errors in the available research client; they are recorded as references, not presented as visually inspected sources. No third-party images, logos, typefaces, animation, or code are copied into this repository.

## Current DAI VIET Audit

The six supplied Phase 4.3 screenshots and `docs/PHASE4_3_PREMIUM_UI_ENHANCEMENT_REPORT.md` show a consistent implementation problem: the visual language is polished but it prioritizes safe UI framing over a memorable world.

## Reference Studies

### 1. [Diablo IV](https://diablo4.blizzard.com/en-us)

The reference uses a world-first posture: a dark, high-contrast atmosphere, a single iconic subject, restrained copy, and a direct play action. Its likely strength is how art, title treatment, and the promise of a hostile world form one read before navigation begins. The risk for DAI VIET is borrowing its gothic heaviness or making the page feel like an ARPG promo.

**Transferable principle:** commit to one world image and make every interface detail feel subordinate to it. For DAI VIET that means deep forest black, a pale rose signal, and an original prehistoric mark, not gothic imagery.

### 2. [Monster Hunter Wilds](https://www.playstation.com/en-vn/games/monster-hunter-wilds/)

The named PlayStation page could not be rendered by the research client, so no visual claim is made here. Its relevant product lesson is the pairing of a named creature ecosystem with a clear discovery loop: monster, habitat, and player action are one story.

**Transferable principle:** DAI VIET should introduce a living island and its inhabitants rather than arranging generic feature boxes. No Monster Hunter media, naming, or UI treatment is used.

### 3. [Awwwards Games & Entertainment](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/games-entertainment/) and [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/)

Both catalog pages returned reader errors. They remain useful as an index of motion-led and art-led work, but are not evidence for copied layouts.

**Transferable principle:** a distinctive rhythm comes from composition, image sequence, type scale, and transitions together. Borders, glow, and icon density cannot substitute for a creative point of view.

### 4. [Cyberpunk 2077](https://www.cyberpunk.net/us/en/)

Cyberpunk frames its universe as a set of stories, then creates multiple entrances through game, expansion, animation, city, characters, and media. The page architecture makes the world feel broader than a purchase path. The trade-off is density: too much product/news inventory can overwhelm a community portal.

**Transferable principle:** use sections as chapters of one world: arrival, signals, people, and next territory. DAI VIET uses this sequence but keeps only one real action and refuses artificial product inventory.

### 5. [Elden Ring](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/elden-ring/elden-ring)

Elden Ring uses sparse declarative copy, immense environmental imagery, and wide intervals between feature chapters. Its feature content is anchored in places, creatures, and a journey rather than app-like capability labels. That restraint gives the imagery authority, although its dense commerce/module area is not suitable for DAI VIET.

**Transferable principle:** let one sentence and one environmental moment carry a section. DAI VIET will use concise Vietnamese writing and field-note labels, avoiding technical status copy in its marketing layer.

### 6. [The Last of Us Part II](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/the-last-of-us-part-ii/)

The PlayStation page establishes emotional stakes first, then deepens the world through story, gameplay, and place-based media. Image sequences do the explanatory work; small textual headings organize rather than decorate. It also exposes a substantial accessibility inventory rather than treating accessibility as an afterthought.

**Transferable principle:** roster and server data should be a human signal inside an emotional composition, not the subject of a system table. DAI VIET keeps semantic headings, keyboard controls, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.

### 7. [Senua's Saga: Hellblade II](https://senuassaga.com/)

Hellblade's official page presents one severe journey with an exceptionally narrow visual and copy focus. It proves that a recognisable atmosphere can be made from restraint: central subject, quiet type, controlled motion, and a simple call to action. Its weakness for a community site would be a lack of breadth.

**Transferable principle:** give the hero a clear silhouette and leave intentional negative space around it. DAI VIET needs its own prehistoric signal, not a busy HUD, in that space.

### 8. [Black Myth: Wukong](https://branding.heishenhua.com/)

The official brand page foregrounds identity and accolades with very little UI noise. It shows how a culturally specific visual language can be carried by a title treatment, material cues, and a considered visual center. The danger is turning that into a generic dark-myth imitation.

**Transferable principle:** DAI VIET's distinction must be authored through its own mark, natural material palette, and Vietnamese community voice. A local forest and a proprietary claw-island motif are more credible than imported mythology.

### 9. [Death Stranding 2: On the Beach](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/death-stranding-2-on-the-beach/)

The official product framing positions an unusual world through a singular visual proposition and a deliberately paced reveal. It demonstrates that a premium game brand does not need dense chrome to feel technological or contemporary.

**Transferable principle:** apply motion and visual devices sparingly. In DAI VIET, a slow organic reveal and a responsive silhouette are enough; no loading screen, simulated map, or elaborate HUD is needed.

## Research Synthesis

The strongest shared pattern is not a color or a card style. It is a hierarchy:

1. Introduce a world with one recognisable focal object.
2. Let media and spacing establish emotion before feature inventory.
3. Treat chapters as narrative transitions, not repeated containers.
4. Make interaction serve a real decision.
5. On mobile, protect the subject and reading order instead of shrinking a desktop composition.

DAI VIET adopts these methods without copying any reference's assets or composition. The resulting direction is `THE ISLAND`: a contemporary community portal with an original claw-island mark, a forest world, rose-smoke signal color, editorial field rolls, and an honest data boundary.
