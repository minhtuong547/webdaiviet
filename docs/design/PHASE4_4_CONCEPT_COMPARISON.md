Scores are comparative design judgments, not product metrics. A score of five means the direction clearly supports the stated DAI VIET constraint; it does not claim user testing, performance telemetry, or ownership approval.

## A: DAI VIET - The Lost World

**Visual premise:** a cinematic prehistoric forest with a large raptor silhouette breaking through mist. Copy is spare, the server signal lives in the horizon line, and the first section reads like a trail into the island.

**Strength:** immediate atmosphere and the strongest dinosaur-centered opening.

**Weakness:** it depends heavily on high-end original art. With only the permitted forest asset available, it risks becoming a familiar dark-forest landing page. It also has the highest image-performance sensitivity.

**Prototype route:** `/design-lab/lost-world`

## B: DAI VIET - Survival Archive

**Visual premise:** an expedition journal made of weathered paper tones, field annotations, species traces, and a collectible archive seal. Content steps through a vertical chronological record.

**Strength:** distinctive editorial personality and a natural way to show constrained data.

**Weakness:** the archive language can regress into a technical system or make real community data feel distant. It is better as a future editorial layer than the public homepage's main language.

**Prototype route:** `/design-lab/survival-archive`

## C: DAI VIET - The Island

**Visual premise:** a luxury, contemporary portal where the forest is an arrival point and the DAI VIET claw-island mark becomes the recurring signature. Sections flow as open landscapes rather than cards: arrival, island signals, names on the island, and future trails.

**Strength:** the best balance of identity, real data, readable Vietnamese copy, accessibility, and mobile composition. The brand mark makes it recognisable even when the title is absent. It does not require fake product previews.

**Weakness:** it must be disciplined. Reintroducing grids, framed panels, badges, or excessive dark chrome would undermine the direction immediately.

**Prototype route:** `/design-lab/the-island`

## Selection

`C: THE ISLAND` is selected for the production homepage. It is materially different from the Phase 4.3 dashboard/bento composition while retaining the same verified API boundaries and working `/may-chu` path. The Lost World prototype remains a useful art benchmark and Survival Archive remains a viable future campaign/editorial mode; neither is inserted into public navigation.
